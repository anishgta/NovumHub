Our Mission

At Army Institute of Technology, students constantly innovate—building robotics projects, developing software, conducting laboratory experiments, and identifying areas for campus enhancement.

Hackrec was built to provide a structured, transparent, and democratic space where:

  • Ideas are nurtured: High-impact project and event proposals receive faculty visibility and peer backing.
  • Research is shared: Student research findings and methodology are highlighted beyond classroom walls.
  • Integrity is upheld: Factual or typographical errors in syllabus, college research publications, or lab manuals can be respectfully submitted and corrected with verified citations.
  • Infrastructure is improved: Practical, actionable campus suggestions (labs, WiFi, hostels, library, safety) are triaged by administration.

Submission Guidelines

Please review these standards before submitting a contribution to ensure quick approval:

Innovative Ideas

Describe the problem your project solves, who benefits in the AIT community, and what resources/guidance you need to bring it to life.

Research Findings

Clearly state your experimental methodology and quantitative results. Attach your draft paper or summary PDF whenever possible.

Research & Academic Corrections

Mandatory Evidence: Clearly identify the college document/manual/paper being corrected, explain exactly what is erroneous, state the correct fact, and provide a verifiable academic source or DOI.

Infrastructure Suggestions

Provide the precise campus location (e.g. Mech Lab 2, D-Hostel 3rd floor), articulate the problem objectively, and propose a viable remedy. Attach a photo if helpful.
